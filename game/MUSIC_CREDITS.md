# Music credits

The seven regional scores are original compositions for Coral Classroom. Their sampled instruments use FluidR3 General MIDI, rendered through FluidSynth. The November 13 arrangements use 32-bar A/A′/B/A″ forms, developed eight-bar phrases, contrasting harmony, and quiet answering lines. Each region keeps its own instrumental palette; the Lighthouse is in 3/4. The pre-rendered scores are normalized near −23 LUFS and streamed by the game. Coral Cove’s existing day and night recordings are preserved.
