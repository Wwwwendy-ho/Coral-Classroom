## 1. Target visual direction

**Art style:** Whimsical, handcrafted 3D storybook.

**Atmosphere:** Magical, enchanting, colorful, immersive, and welcoming.

**Character design:** You have permission to redesign characters substantially while preserving their identities and personalities.

**Animation:** A combination of natural underwater movement and expressive, playful reactions.

**Performance:** Balanced. Maintain smooth performance on iPads and iPhones while providing higher-quality visuals on more powerful devices.

**Asset creation:** Prefer original assets created procedurally with Blender, Python scripting, and custom modeling techniques. Avoid paid assets or services unless I approve them.

### Visual characteristics

- Beautifully sculpted, organic character shapes.
- Soft curves and refined silhouettes rather than visibly assembled spheres, cylinders, and boxes.
- Expressive faces and appealing proportions.
- Detailed but cohesive shells, fins, tails, eyes, and other character features.
- Hand-painted-looking textures and carefully controlled materials.
- Soft underwater lighting, subtle bioluminescence, and atmospheric depth.
- Rich coral gardens, sea plants, shells, rocks, and environmental details.
- Harmonious turquoise, lavender, seafoam, coral pink, peach, and warm gold.
- Distinctive environments that share one consistent artistic identity.

Avoid photorealism, excessively shiny plastic materials, crude low-poly geometry, harsh lighting, overwhelming bloom, and excessive visual clutter.

The objective is not maximum geometric complexity. It is professional artistic quality.

- Characters look organically sculpted rather than assembled from primitives.

### Animation quality

- Smooth transitions without snapping.

### World quality

- All regions share one coherent artistic identity.
- Lighting and materials complement the character designs.

### Technical quality

- No broken models or materials.
- No regressions in educational functionality.
- No major performance degradation.
- Offline and reduced-motion modes continue working.
- Automated tests pass, or failures are explicitly documented.

The target visual scores are subjective review goals, not something that can be proven by automated tests alone.
