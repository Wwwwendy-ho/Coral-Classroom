# Cast concept survey

This folder preserves original concept sheets for a single survey of the rest of Coral Classroom's cast. The approved Shelly appearance is the style reference and is not being reconsidered.

There are 17 cast identities and 18 design reviews because Kiko has both Mochi and Hermit forms. Each row has three options, A, B and C from left to right. `survey-manifest.json` maps every review ID to its row, sheet, role, species and option descriptions. The recurring pufferfish merchant Bok is distinct from the Harbor Town octopus shopkeeper.

These are generated 3D-style concept illustrations, not exported 3D models or animation previews. The initial survey has no selected defaults. The owner's completed choices and custom teenage-Mira direction are recorded in `preferences-2026-11-16.json`; `CAST-DIRECTION.md` applies the request for distinct personalities within the same art style. Future models must be reviewed from multiple actual viewable angles and within the mobile performance budget.

The survey source and compact embedded delivery are in `/workspace/work/character-survey/`. The originals here remain untouched; JPEG copies are compressed for the conversation only. No concept images are loaded by the game.
