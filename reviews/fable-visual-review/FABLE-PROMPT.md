# Prompt to give Fable

Please critique Coral Classroom’s visuals and proposed work using this review folder. Start with README.md, GAME-CAPTURES.md, WORLD-COMPARISONS.md, PLANNED-WORK.md and the source guide. Open the images at full size rather than judging only Markdown thumbnails.

The owner wants a vivid pastel, polished 3D marine board/RPG world, with natural animal characters the player talks to while learning Korean. All characters must be marine organisms. Keep the chosen A-eye visual design, but let each personality change its gaze, lids where appropriate, attention and timing. Use natural species-specific mouths rather than human smiles. Echo is a beluga, Pip a royal gramma, and the taxi squid is bored.

The highest constraint is smooth performance on an iPhone XS. The strongest detail belongs on the travel tiles and nearest interaction landmarks. We want believable shape, texture and shading without costly geometric density, transparency layers, live shadows or heavy postprocessing.

Please address:

1. What are the five biggest visual weaknesses in the evidence? Cite a specific image, region or source subsystem for each.
2. Do the character and world treatments belong together? Which contours, materials, color relationships and baked lighting choices would close the quality gap most efficiently?
3. Can you recognize every region from its landmark silhouette and spatial composition, rather than color alone? Propose one strong improvement per region while retaining closed routes and readable tile play.
4. Are the camera height, landscape breadth, foreground/background hierarchy and interaction labels clear? Give concrete board, travel-overview and dialogue-camera recommendations.
5. Which new surface details read as natural material, and which become noise or repetition? Inspect the before/after pairs. Would you change atlas scale, contrast, coverage or the balance of material detail versus contour detail?
6. Are the planned character silhouettes, biological palettes and natural expressions distinct enough? How should each personality influence eye acting without humanizing the face?
7. Where is the plan spending too much rendering/loading budget? Consider added surface attributes, batch splits, transparent screen coverage, texture memory, resident LOD and lifecycle. Static CPU counts do not establish device FPS.
8. Reorder PLANNED-WORK.md into a practical sequence. For the top five changes give the visual benefit, specific implementation, estimated cost category, and a clear way to judge success. Identify work that should wait.

Keep evidence categories separate: November 15 full browser captures, current offline scenery-only renders, generated character concepts, existing model renders, and proposed features. The offline white/orange disks are not the real tiles, and omitted characters/props/fog should not be judged as missing from the actual game. The repository root is an older public build; source-snapshot contains the selected current visual code. Please separate confirmed observations from inferences and ask for extra evidence where needed.

Fable’s LEARNING_DESIGN.md and ADAPTIVE_TEACHER.md are also present under the repository’s docs/. They guide the learning roadmap, not evidence that those systems have shipped. Consider their implications for calm dialogue space, accessible controls and short travel, while keeping this review focused on visuals.
