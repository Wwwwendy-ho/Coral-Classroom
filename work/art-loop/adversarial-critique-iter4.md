## 3. Forensic Root-Cause Log (Verified in Live Source Code)

1. **Coral Cove Invisible Backface-Culled Palm Fronds (`crafted-world.js` L222, L253, L650–656)**: `painted` is a single-sided `MeshBasicMaterial({ vertexColors: true })`. In `palmTree()`, `u` runs transversely (`[-sn, cs]`) while `v` runs radially outward (`[cs, sn]`), so `dP/du × dP/dv` points downward (`-Y`). Calling `panelGrid(4, 7, ..., parent, false)` winds every frond face clockwise from above — **backface-culling 100% of every frond crown from the gameplay camera!**
2. **Coral Cove Sailboat Impaling Conch Pavilion (`crafted-world.js` L495)**: `heroBoatSpot = findStartBandSpot(..., 2.0, 4.6)` passes `siteGap = 4.6`, which is smaller than the conch pavilion radius (`~3.8`) plus sailboat half-length (`1.9` = `5.7`), impaling the pavilion directly.
3. **Coral Cove Jagged Sawtooth White Foam (`crafted-world.js` L664–686)**: `atollIslet()` uses only 2 radial bands (`v = 0, 0.5, 1.0`) with sharp `7+11`-lobe scallop amplitude right near the animated/undulating water plane (`yBand = +0.025..+0.040`), causing harsh diagonal z-intersection teeth against the caustic water surface, compounded by 5 flat unlit `#ffffff` detached puff ellipsoids.
4. **Harbor Town Giant Crate Collisions & Emptied Basin (`board.js` L949–963 & `region-scenery.js` L1026–1034)**: `regionProps.town = ['crate']` spawns `MODEL_FOR.crate` (`crate` / `crateBottles` at `1.1 * 1.25 = 1.375` scale) at fixed tile offsets (`s.x + side * 2.1`) with zero clearance checks against `region-scenery.js` streetlamps, seawall railings, or fishing piers. Meanwhile, `waterClear(x, z, 4.5)` against the central ferry (`5.5`) and fishing piers (`5.2`) rejected every single basin sailboat candidate.
5. **Lighthouse Cottage Slicing Keeper Hana (`region-scenery.js` L1464–1486)**: Pulling `cotR` inward moved `(cotX, cotZ)` directly onto Keeper Hana's host marker location next to `main[0]`/`main[1]`, because `outerClear(cotX, cotZ, 4.6)` only checks tile centers, never `view.regionPeople.marker` or the `~5.4×4.6` cottage yard footprint.
6. **Hotel Whale Dorsal Shark-Fin Pole Split (`build_town_residents.py` L530)**: `z = .82 + .24 * (back ** 2.2) + .50 * cv * (1.0 - .82 * (back ** 2.2) * (sv ** 2))` leaves `.24 * (back ** 2.2)` active even when `sv = 0` (`v = 0` top pole), turning the top pole into a vertical razor crest rising from `z = 1.32` (front) to `z = 1.56` (back) directly behind the bellhop cap!
7. **Pharmacy Shrimp Invisible Dorsal Carapace Plates (`build_town_residents.py` L643)**: `plate = .16 * shingle * math.sin(math.pi * pv) * sv * dorsal` multiplies by `sv = math.sin(math.pi * v)`, which evaluates to **`0.0` along the entire top dorsal ridge (`v = 0`)** where overlapping shell plates need to break the silhouette!
8. **Otter & Captain Bay Clashing Orange Iris Goggles (`build_town_residents.py` L81–83 & `build_supporting_hosts.py` L29–33)**: Even with `rim_scale = 0.0` on Otter, both scripts paint the large iris ring (`r = 0.74..1.18`) vivid bright orange (`(174,96,36)/(248,178,92)` and `(212,134,46)/(255,214,120)`), reading as glowing safety-orange goggles instead of `ref-ocean-cast.jpg`'s deep glossy espresso-dark eyes.
9. **Captain Bay Pitch-Black Hind Flipper & Visor-Clipping Eyebrow (`build_supporting_hosts.py` L290, L312, L323)**: L312 uses `reverse=layer<0` without multiplying by `s` (flipping normals inside-out on one side!), and L290 places the white eyebrow at `z = 2.045, y = -.395` straight through the lowered cap visor (`z = 2.02` at `y = -.36..-0.68`).
10. **Kiko Close GLB Budget Overrun & Unchanged `resident-models.js`**: Rebuilding `build_kiko_cast.py` at JPEG quality `90` bumped `kiko-hermit-close.glb` to `1549.2 KB` and `kiko-mochi-close.glb` to `1466.3 KB` (both `> 1433.6 KB`). `resident-models.js` L84–88 was untouched (`regionResidentInFlight` boolean lock).
