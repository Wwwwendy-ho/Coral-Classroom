# Anatomy review at each character stage

The owner flagged a six-legged appearance in Shelly's loading artwork. Treat anatomy as a recurring acceptance check at concept creation, asset construction, animation review and screen integration. A correct rig count does not prove a rendered silhouette reads correctly.

For each concept, trace each visible appendage back to its attachment. Mark hidden appendages as occluded rather than inventing a visible count. Reject detached limbs, branching arms, duplicate fins, ambiguous shell edges and accessories that resemble extra limbs. Review front, rear and both sides for models; check idle, attentive, reaction and travel poses, plus the loading, board and dialogue crops. Check low-detail assets separately. Keep living bodies distinct from crafted scenery.
