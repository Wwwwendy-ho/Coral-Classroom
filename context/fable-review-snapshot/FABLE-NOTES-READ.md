These cover learning architecture and adaptive teaching; LEARNING_DESIGN explicitly excludes boards, art and music. They do not replace the owner’s visual selections or prove those proposed systems are implemented.

The learning plan proposes stable content IDs and migration, one FSRS scheduler with channel-specific evidence, a Harbor Town output ladder, more listen-first input, fluency, assessment and a reviewable content pipeline. The adaptive teacher proposes local rule-based adjustments, evidence thresholds, bounds, pinned settings, undo, supportive gap handling and no reminders or notifications.

Implications for the visual work: reserve clear screen space for Korean/audio/word-building/typed answers; keep the resident visible during conversations; keep travel short; avoid decorative UI taking learning time; make teacher notes readable and optional to act on. The implementation roadmaps remain distinct from this current visual review. They are advice supplied by Fable, evaluated under the owner’s request and priorities.
