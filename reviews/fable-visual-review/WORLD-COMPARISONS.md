# Current world pass — actual scenery, offline

These are CPU/Blender inspections of geometry generated by the game’s production scenery constructors, with existing baked vertex colors and the actual texture atlas. Every before/after pair shares its camera. No new artistic lighting was added. They expose the material/shape change, not the appearance of a complete running game.

**Important:** the white/orange disks are simple route-position references, not the game’s travel tiles. Characters, full board assets, UI, route ribbon, imported GLTF decorations and distant outskirts are omitted. Cove shows only its extra sculpted scenery, not the complete reef. Distance fog is omitted so source geometry is visible. Region exports use the actual procedural fallback prop kit. See [method](evidence/OFFLINE-RENDER-METHOD.md) and [source/camera manifest](evidence/render-manifest.json).
